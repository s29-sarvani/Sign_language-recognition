How it works

From camera to text in three steps

A convolutional neural network built with TensorFlow, trained on thousands of labelled hand-sign photos.

1

Capture

Your browser reads the webcam and crops the area inside the detection box several times per second.

2

Recognise

The cropped image is sent to the local model, which scores every sign and returns the most likely matches.

3

Stabilise

Predictions are smoothed over recent frames so a letter appears only when it is held steadily, then added to your text.
